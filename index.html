Biography

Ce Wang is an Associate Professor in the School of Science at Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU). Previously, he was a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, working with Prof. Xiaomeng Li. Before that, he was a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, working with Prof. S. Kevin Zhou at the MIRACLE Research Group.

He received his Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Nankai University under the supervision of Prof. Yongchuan Chen and Prof. Weidong Gao. He also collaborates with Dr. You Hao, Dr. Kun Shang, Dr. Huaming Wu, and Dr. Tianli Liao.
